🎉 Event Ratio Benchmarks

  • Catering (~45%): Quality food & beverages constitute the primary guest experience.
  • Venue (~30%): Covers climate control, dedicated space, seating, and restrooms.
  • Decor & Lighting (~15%): Transforms ambiance and ensures photogenic memories.
  • Sound & Music (~10%): Crisp announcements, party playlists, and speaker clarity.

💡 Practical Planning Checklist

Verify whether service staff, cleanup, and ice supply are bundled in your catering quote to prevent surprise day-of expenses.
