Distribute your home budget across furniture, lighting, ventilation, and decor.

💡 How Budget Distribution Works

  • Core Furniture (~50%): Anchor seating, tables, and primary storage.
  • Layered Lighting (~15%): Diffused task and ambient illumination to prevent eye fatigue.
  • Ventilation & Fixtures (~15%): High-efficiency BLDC motors reducing lifetime electricity.
  • Soft Furnishings (~10%): Curtains, acoustic rugs, and tactile accents.
  • Surplus Buffer (~10%): Covers delivery, hardware, and installation margins.
